The first release of MzPCF: the pattern-controlled filter from ReBirth RB-338, as a VST3 effect.

- **32 patterns**, struck on every sixteenth of your project, sample-accurately, through loops and tempo changes.
- **FREQ, Q, AMT, DEC** and **LP/BP**, as on ReBirth's panel, plus **MIX** and **LEVEL** for use as an insert.
- The same PCF as MzDebirth's effects rack: a pattern number means the same in both.

Known limits:
- **Windows only** for now.
- **Not measured against ReBirth.** The filter and envelope follow ReBirth's manual; the ranges, curves and patterns are chosen by ear.
- **Plays only while your DAW plays.** Stopped, the filter rests at FREQ.
